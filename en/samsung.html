Before you start

What you need

A Samsung TV Tizen 2.4 or newer. Works on old and recent models.
An Android phone Android 7 or newer. The installer is downloaded to the phone, not the TV.
The same Wi-Fi The phone and the TV must be on the same local network.

Step by step

Install Astel on the TV

It takes a few minutes. The installer downloads, signs and installs the right package for your model.

Download the APK Open the link on your phone and allow installing apps from this source if Android asks.
Enable developer mode On the TV: Settings → Support → About this TV → tap “Contact info” or the version 5 times. Enable “Developer mode” and restart.
Same network and IP Connect the phone and the TV to the same Wi-Fi. If the TV asks for a developer IP, enter the one shown by the installer (the phone's IP).
Open the installer Tap “Find TVs” and choose your Samsung from the list.
Tap Install The installer downloads Astel 0.9.0, signs it for your TV and installs it. On older TVs (Tizen 2.x/3.x) it uses the compatible package.
Open Astel on the TV Find Astel in the TV app list and enter your media service address, username and password.

You can minimise the installer while it works: it keeps installing and shows progress in the notification.

If something fails

Common issues

My TV does not appear in the list

Check that the phone and TV are on the same Wi-Fi, that developer mode is on and that the TV's developer IP is the phone's. Tap “Find TVs” again.

Install error (install failed[118])

There is usually a previous version of Astel. Uninstall it from the TV app list and try again. On older TVs the installer uses the 2012 public signer.

The TV asks for a Samsung account

Some 2022 and newer models need the developer certificate. The installer guides you through signing in with your Samsung account and requesting it.

Android blocks the APK

That is normal: it is not on Play Store. In the Android warning choose “Install anyway” or allow “Install unknown apps” for the browser or file manager.

How do I update?

Download the new APK, repeat the steps and the installer replaces the version. App data is kept.

Astel is only a player: it does not include or distribute channels, movies, series or playlists. It plays the content you already have access to with your own media service.
