Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Astel is a media player. It does not include any content of its own and does not sell personal data. This policy explains what data the app processes on all its platforms (Samsung TV, LG, Android, Fire TV and Windows).

1. Data stored on the device

  • Media service credentials (server address, username and password): stored only in the app's local storage, so you do not have to type them every time. They are not sent to Astel; they are used to connect to the service you configured.
  • Favourites, history, playback progress and settings: stored on the device.

2. Sync with Astel's server

If you sign in, favourites, history and playback progress are synced with Astel's server so you can get them back on another device. The sync is linked to the media service username and a random device identifier. Neither the password nor the service address is synced.

3. Access log

When you sign in and when you open the app with an active session, the app sends Astel's server: username, device identifier, model, platform, app version, IP address and User-Agent. It is used to manage access and prevent abuse; it is not used for advertising.

4. Diagnostic report (optional)

Under Settings > About there may be a button to send a diagnostic report. It is only sent if you press it. It includes device model, system version, decoders, player status and the last few technical lines. Before sending, username, password, service URL and tokens are removed. It is used only to fix technical problems.

5. Data we do not collect

  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or address.
  • No advertising is used, the advertising ID is not used and data is not sold.
  • Location is not tracked.
  • No access to contacts, photos or files on your device.

6. Third-party services

  • The media service you configure receives your credentials and playback requests, under its own privacy policy.
  • TMDB is used for posters and movie/series details; only title searches are sent, no personal data.

7. Minors and parental control

The app is not aimed at minors. It includes a PIN-based parental control to hide the categories you choose. Available content depends on your media service.

8. Retention and deletion

Local data is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. Synced data can be deleted from the app under Settings > Data > Delete synced data, or by requesting deletion at the contact address. More details on the data deletion page.

9. Contact

For any privacy question: jedohodev00@gmail.com

10. Changes

If this policy changes, a new version will be published at the same URL with the update date.

Astel does not include content of its own. It plays the content you already have access to through your media service.
