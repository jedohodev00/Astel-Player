Delete your data

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Astel does not create accounts: there is no sign-up, no name and no email. The app still stores some data to work. Here is how to delete it.

Contents
  1. What data it stores
  2. Delete it from the app
  3. Delete it from the device
  4. Request deletion by email
  5. Timeframe

1. What data it stores

  • On the device: server, username and password of the service you configure, favourites, history, playback progress and settings.
  • Synced: favourites, history and progress, linked to the media service username and a random device identifier, so you can get them back on another device.

2. Delete it from the app

  1. Open Astel and go to Settings > Data.
  2. Press Delete synced data to delete both the copy on the device and the one stored in your account.
  3. You can also delete only the history or the favourites with their own buttons.

3. Delete it from the device

Uninstall the app or, on Android, go to Android Settings > Apps > Astel > Storage > Clear data. On Samsung and LG, simply uninstall the app from the apps menu.

4. Request deletion by email

If you prefer us to delete it, write to jedohodev00@gmail.com with the subject “Delete data”. Include the device model and the approximate date of use so we can locate the sync.

Request deletion by email

5. Timeframe

Email requests are handled within a maximum of 30 days. No data is kept after deletion.

Astel does not sell data, does not use advertising and does not ask for location, contacts, photos or payment data.
