What Astel is

A player, not a catalogue

Astel does not include any content of its own: it connects to the media service you already subscribe to and shows it with a comfortable interface on any screen.

What it is
  • A cross-platform media player: TV, phone, tablet and PC.
  • An app to use the media service you already have access to.
  • An interface built for the remote: grid, programme guide and a page for every title.
  • Privacy by design: no ads and no trackers.
What it is not
  • It does not host, select or distribute third-party content.
  • It does not sell channels, subscriptions or playlists.
  • It does not ask for personal data, location or access to your files.
  • It does not show ads and there are no in-app purchases.

How it works

Three steps and you are done

FAQ

Common questions

Does Astel include channels or movies?

No. Astel is only the player: the content is provided by the media service you configure. We do not sell or distribute playlists, channels or subscriptions.

Do I need to create an Astel account?

There is no sign-up. The app uses your media service account, which is stored in the device's private storage and is not sent to Astel.

How do I install it on a Samsung or LG TV?

On Samsung and LG you install it from the TV's own app store (Samsung Apps and LG Content Store).

Does it work on Fire TV and Fire Stick?

Yes, on Fire OS devices. The interface switches to TV mode automatically and works with the Fire remote.

How do I install it on Windows?

Download the installer and run it: the app is downloaded during installation and appears in the Start menu, with no administrator rights needed. If Windows shows a SmartScreen warning, click “More info” → “Run anyway”.

Do you collect personal data?

We do not ask for name, email, phone or location. Only favourites, history and progress are synced, linked to your service username and a random device identifier. You can delete them at any time.

Need help?

Write to us about the app or any technical issue. We reply as soon as possible.
